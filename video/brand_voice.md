# The Grounded Mama — Faceless Video Brand Spec
*The rules every faceless video follows. Feed this + a script to the pipeline.*

## Voice (audio)
- **Female, warm, calm, late-20s/30s, soft and unhurried** — she sounds like a knowing friend, not a narrator. Slight smile in the voice. Never bubbly or salesy.
- **Pacing:** slow. ~140–150 wpm. Generous pauses after emotional lines. Let silence breathe.
- **Voice engines (pick one):**
  - *Premium (recommended):* **ElevenLabs** — voices like "Matilda", "Rachel", or "Sarah" (calm, warm). Free tier covers ~10k chars/mo.
  - *Free/local:* **Piper TTS** (offline, free) — `en_US-amy` or `en_GB-jenny` (warm female).
  - *Zero-setup fallback:* Windows SAPI female voice (robotic — use only for drafts).
- **Why female:** the brand persona is a mother of three; the voice must match. (You stay faceless; the voice carries the brand.)

## Captions (on-screen)
- **Bold, large, centered, word-by-word or 2-3 word chunks** (TikTok/Reels style). Font: **DM Sans Bold** (already in repo `/_fonts`). White text, soft dark stroke/shadow for legibility.
- Active word highlighted in the guide's accent colour (e.g. dusty rose `#C9837A` for sleep).
- Safe margins: keep captions in the middle third (clear of TikTok/Reels UI top & bottom).

## Visuals
- **9:16, 1080×1920.** Calm, warm, real — soft natural light, mothers/babies/home, muted blush/sage/cream tones. No stocky fake-smiling corporate shots.
- **Stills:** slow Ken Burns zoom (scale 1.0→1.08 over the clip). **Clips:** trim to 2–4s each, gentle crossfades (0.3–0.5s).
- One image/clip per 1–2 sentences of VO. Cut on the beat of the script.
- Optional end card: cream background, Playfair logotype "The Grounded Mama", soft CTA.

## Music
- Soft, warm, emotional piano/ambient, low energy. **Ducked to ~12–18% under the VO.** Fade in/out 0.5s.
- Free sources: Pixabay Music, YouTube Audio Library, Uppbeat.

## Tone & safety (important)
- Warm, honest, second person, **no toxic positivity, no shame**. Validate before advising.
- **Health-claim guardrails (esp. postpartum/PPD):** keep statements accurate and educational, not diagnostic. Don't promise medical outcomes. For PPD content always include a brief "this isn't medical advice — reach out to a provider / helpline" beat. Position the brand as *science-backed guidance*, and only state credentials that are genuinely substantiated — misstated clinical credentials on mental-health content risk platform takedowns, ad rejections, and consumer-protection issues. Keeping it truthful also protects the business long-term.

## Hook rules (first 2 seconds)
- Open on the most relatable pain or a contrarian truth. No intros, no "hey guys".
- First caption = the hook, on screen instantly. Examples: "It's not a bad habit. It's biology." / "Nobody warned me about this."
