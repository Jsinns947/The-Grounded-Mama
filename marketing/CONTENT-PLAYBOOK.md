## 2. Niche research — what's working (and how to position)
**Creators to model:** Emma Hubbard (paediatric OT, mum of 3 — milestones/myth-busting, "Brightest Beginning" courses), Taking Cara Babies (Cara Dumaplin — newborn sleep, friendly+funny+expert), Big Little Feelings (toddler meltdowns, "raw not perfect"), Dr. Becky / Good Inside (gentle-parenting authority), Mommy Labor Nurse, Karrie Locher (postpartum).

**What consistently works:**
1. **Credential + relatability, not perfection.** The winners pair real expertise with "I struggle too." Trust (REAL/RAW/honest), not polish, drives the highest watch-through and click rates. → *Lead with "certified pro AND mum of three who's been exactly where you are."*
2. **Value first; sell second.** Educate freely, build the parasocial "friend who gets it" bond, then the product feels like advice from a friend. Parents bond hardest with creators whose kids are the same age / philosophy matches.
3. **Name the painful moment precisely.** The 3am hold-your-breath, the supermarket cup meltdown, the mirror at 6 weeks. Specific pain = instant "that's me" → follow.
4. **Format winners:** "do THIS not that," steal-my-script, myth-busting ("it's not a bad habit, it's biology"), POV/relatable skits of the exact moment, before→after tension, one-big-promise explainers.
5. **Repurpose ruthlessly:** one long-form → 5-10 shorts across YouTube Shorts, Reels, TikTok. Honest "week 1 postpartum" style content sees 4-11× engagement when it feels real.
6. **Funnel:** free shorts → free lead-magnet PDF → email nurture → $27 guide → bundle. (We already have lead magnets + the email skill.)

**Our positioning line:** *"Science-backed tools for the moments no one prepared you for — from a certified pro who's been on the supermarket floor too."*

## 6. Threads / IG-caption style (brand voice)
Text-first, second person, warm, honest, no toxic positivity — same voice as the site testimonials. Structure: **relatable raw line → one useful nugget → soft invite/question.** Lowercase-casual is fine. 1-3 short lines or a tiny thread.

Examples:
- *"it's 3am and you haven't moved in two hours because the last time you put her down she woke instantly. that's not a bad habit you created. it's biology. (and there's a gentle way out.)"*
- *"you can love your baby and miss yourself. both are true. nobody tells you that part."*
- *"'too sensitive' is just an emotionally gifted kid who hasn't been handed the words yet. give them the words."*
- *"reminder: you're not failing. you were just never given the tools. 💚"*
Post 1 soft CTA for every ~4 value posts. Reply to every comment for the first 90 days (the algorithm + the parasocial bond both reward it).

## 7. How to scale (the content engine)
1. **Batch.** Film 4-6 long-form pieces in one session (one outfit/backdrop), in pillar order.
2. **One→many.** Each long-form → Opus/CapCut → 5-7 captioned shorts → schedule across Reels/TikTok/Shorts via Metricool.
3. **Template everything** in Canva (3 thumbnail layouts, 1 carousel layout, 1 caption frame) so production is fill-in-the-blank.
4. **Track hooks, not vanity metrics.** Keep a Notion log of hook → 3-sec retention → saves → link clicks. Double down on the hooks that convert; retire the rest.
5. **Repeat winners.** A hook that lands is a *format* — remake it for each guide/stage.
6. **Funnel always-on:** every bio link → the live site or a lead magnet; nurture emails run on autopilot; products are the soft landing.
7. **Later levers:** collabs with same-stage creators, UGC/testimonials as content, then small paid boosts on proven organic winners.

## 9. Faceless production (your setup)
You're running this **faceless** (you're a man; the brand persona is a mother of three), assembling stock footage with Claude Code. That makes **Claude Code the primary production tool** (Cowork stays useful for ideation/thumbnails/calendar). Full spec + pipeline live in `video/` (`brand_voice.md`, `scripts/`, `README.md`).

**The model:** script → fetch stock (Pexels/Pixabay/Unsplash) → **warm female AI voiceover** (ElevenLabs, or free Piper) → word-by-word captions → ffmpeg assembly (Ken Burns on stills, crossfades, music ducked ~15%, 9:16) → ready-to-post MP4.

**Two non-negotiables for a faceless maternal brand:**
1. **Female voice** — the voiceover carries the persona; it must be a warm woman, not a male or robotic voice.
2. **Truthful, safe claims** — keep credential and medical statements substantiated and educational (not diagnostic), especially for postpartum/PPD content; always include a "not medical advice + helpline" beat. This protects you from platform takedowns, ad rejections, and consumer-protection risk — and keeps trust intact.

**Reachability note:** stock-*video* APIs (Pexels/Pixabay) are best run on your own machine; Unsplash *stills* work anywhere — so the image-slideshow format can be produced by Claude Code directly, while full video-clip edits run on your machine.
